In a manual-guidance task with five participants, high constant damping yielded significantly lower path-length error (e) than low constant damping. Execution time (H) was shorter with low constant damping, although the difference was not statistically significant. Instructions to prioritize accuracy may have contributed to variability in execution time, but this explanation was not tested.
