**English abstract**

We propose the Stable Estimator of Dynamical Systems (SEDS) for learning globally stable models of point-to-point robot motion from demonstrations. A Gaussian mixture model describes the joint distribution of positions and velocities, and Gaussian mixture regression yields a nonlinear autonomous dynamical system. The resulting motion model is a state-dependent weighted combination of local linear dynamical systems. SEDS optimizes the model parameters to fit the demonstrations under constraints requiring the local systems to share a goal and the symmetric parts of their system matrices to be negative definite. These constraints guarantee global asymptotic stability of the learned dynamical system at the goal, so that position converges to the goal from any initial model state and velocity tends to zero. Motion generation depends on the current state and has no prescribed time schedule, allowing the model to respond to spatial and temporal perturbations during execution. We evaluate the method in simulations of human handwriting motions and experiments with iCub and Katana-T robot arms. The robot experiments demonstrate reproduction of the demonstrated position and velocity patterns and motion to the goal from different starting positions. In the Katana-T experiments, moving the target during execution causes the generated trajectory to adjust towards the new goal without relearning the model parameters.

**中文翻译**

我们提出稳定动力系统估计器（SEDS），用于从示范中学习全局稳定的机器人点到点运动模型。高斯混合模型描述位置与速度的联合分布，高斯混合回归由此得到非线性自治动力系统。所得运动模型是多个局部线性动力系统的加权组合，其权重随当前状态变化。SEDS 在约束条件下优化模型参数以拟合示范，这些约束要求各局部系统具有共同目标，且各系统矩阵的对称部分负定。这些约束保证学习到的动力系统在目标处全局渐近稳定，使位置从任意模型初始状态收敛到目标，速度趋于零。运动生成依赖当前状态，不受预设时间进度约束，因此模型能够在执行过程中响应空间和时间扰动。我们通过人类手写运动的模拟以及 iCub 和 Katana-T 机械臂实验评价该方法。机器人实验展示了对示范中位置及速度特点的再现，以及从不同初始位置生成到达目标的运动。在 Katana-T 实验中，执行期间移动目标会使生成的轨迹相应调整并朝新目标运动，无需重新学习模型参数。

**正文外说明：**写作以 A06、A02 为主要参考，A07 补充验证表达。全局渐近稳定性保证针对学习到的动力系统；真实机器人执行仍依赖底层控制器及其输入—状态稳定性假设。时间扰动响应表述为模型能力，未写成实验验证结论。
