**English abstract**

This paper proposes an impedance learning method that adjusts time-varying damping and stiffness through repeated interaction between rigid robot manipulators and unknown, time-varying environments, while keeping the desired inertia fixed. Gradient following and iterative improvement are integrated in the outer loop to reduce a weighted interaction-error cost that specifies the relative importance of trajectory tracking and tracking the time integral of interaction force. The iterative improvement scheme is derived for a linear time-varying environment model and uses interaction feedback to avoid precise identification of environment parameters. An adaptive position controller is designed for the robot to track the virtual reference trajectory generated by the target impedance, thereby realizing the learned impedance without requiring a robot dynamics regressor. For the considered robot dynamics, theoretical analysis establishes asymptotic tracking of the virtual reference trajectory and bounded closed-loop signals when the reference velocity and acceleration are bounded and the control and adaptive gains are suitably chosen. Numerical simulation results demonstrate task-cost improvement for two choices of the trajectory-tracking weight. Experiments on repeated interaction between a robot wrist and a stationary human hand demonstrate task-dependent adjustment of the trade-off between trajectory-tracking accuracy and compliance.

**中文翻译**

本文提出一种阻抗学习方法，通过刚性机械臂与未知时变环境之间的重复交互，调整随时间变化的阻尼和刚度，同时保持期望惯性不变。外环将梯度跟随与迭代改进相结合，以降低加权交互误差代价，该代价规定轨迹跟踪与交互力时间积分跟踪的相对重要性。迭代改进方案基于线性时变环境模型推导，并利用交互反馈避免对环境参数进行精确辨识。自适应位置控制器用于使机器人跟踪由目标阻抗生成的虚拟参考轨迹，从而实现所学阻抗，无需机器人动力学回归矩阵。对于所考虑的机器人动力学，理论分析证明，在参考速度和加速度有界且控制增益与自适应增益选取适当的条件下，机器人渐近跟踪虚拟参考轨迹，闭环信号有界。数值仿真结果表明，两种轨迹跟踪权重设置下的任务代价均得到改善。机器人腕部与位置固定的人手之间的重复交互实验表明，该方法能够根据任务要求调整轨迹跟踪精度与柔顺性之间的取舍。
