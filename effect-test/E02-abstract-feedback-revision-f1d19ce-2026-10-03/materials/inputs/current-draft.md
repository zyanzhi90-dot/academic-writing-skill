**English abstract**

This paper proposes an impedance learning method for rigid robot manipulators interacting with unknown, time-varying environments. The outer loop combines gradient following and iterative improvement to update time-varying damping and stiffness from interaction feedback, while the desired inertia remains fixed. A weighted interaction-error cost specifies the relative importance of trajectory tracking and tracking the time integral of interaction force. The impedance updates are derived using a linear time-varying environment model without requiring exact environment parameters. An adaptive position controller is designed for the robot to track the virtual reference trajectory generated by the target impedance without requiring a robot dynamics regressor. Theoretical analysis establishes asymptotic tracking of the virtual reference trajectory and bounded closed-loop signals for the considered robot dynamics, bounded reference derivatives and suitably chosen control and adaptive gains. Numerical simulation results demonstrate task-cost improvement for two choices of the trajectory-tracking weight. Experiments on repeated interaction between a robot wrist and a stationary human hand show that a higher trajectory-tracking weight increases stiffness and reduces position error, whereas a lower weight reduces stiffness and produces more compliant motion.

**中文翻译**

本文提出一种阻抗学习方法，用于刚性机械臂与未知时变环境之间的物理交互。外环将梯度跟随与迭代改进相结合，根据交互反馈更新随时间变化的阻尼和刚度，同时保持期望惯性不变。加权交互误差代价规定轨迹跟踪与交互力时间积分跟踪的相对重要性。阻抗更新基于线性时变环境模型推导，无需环境参数的精确值。自适应位置控制器用于使机器人跟踪由目标阻抗生成的虚拟参考轨迹，无需机器人动力学回归矩阵。理论分析证明，对于所考虑的机器人动力学，在参考轨迹导数有界且控制增益与自适应增益选取适当的条件下，机器人渐近跟踪虚拟参考轨迹，闭环信号有界。数值仿真结果表明，两种轨迹跟踪权重设置下的任务代价均得到改善。机器人腕部与位置固定的人手之间的重复交互实验表明，较高的轨迹跟踪权重使刚度增大、位置误差减小，而较低的权重使刚度减小、运动更加柔顺。
