# Section: independent Related Work (polishing)

Use this fragment when the draft has a separate Related Work section. When
literature is part of Introduction, use `section/intro.md` instead. Preserve
the target venue's section arrangement.

- Find the technical axis of each comparison group and check whether the
  group, as a whole, establishes prior capability, applicable conditions,
  remaining gap, and why that gap matters to the present design or test.
  Individual paragraphs need only do their actual share. Do not manufacture
  a limitation or transition at every paragraph end.
- Repair missing comparison criteria, unsupported dismissals of prior work,
  or a distinction that describes only the absence of the author's method.
  Keep attribution and citations tied to the actual prior claim.
- For robotics manuscripts, use the conditionally loaded shared robotics body
  reference to check sensing, interaction partner, control objective,
  guarantee, contact condition, or evaluation regime. Preserve terms that
  identify genuinely different assumptions or system roles.

Polish the smallest span that fixes the comparison. If a technical distinction
cannot be recovered from the supplied manuscript or sources, flag the missing
information rather than writing one into the literature.
