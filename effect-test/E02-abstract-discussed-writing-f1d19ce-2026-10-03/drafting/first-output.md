**英文摘要**

This paper proposes an impedance learning method for rigid manipulators to adjust the trade-off between trajectory tracking accuracy and compliance through repeated interaction. For environments modelled as unknown linear time-varying systems, gradient following and iterative improvement are combined to learn time-varying damping and stiffness from a weighted interaction-error cost without precise identification of the environment parameters. An adaptive position controller is designed to realize the learned impedance by tracking the virtual reference trajectory generated by the target impedance. The controller does not require a regressor matrix for the robot dynamics. For the considered manipulator dynamics, theoretical analysis establishes asymptotic tracking of the virtual reference trajectory and bounded closed-loop signals when the reference velocity and acceleration are bounded and the control gains and adaptation parameters satisfy the theorem conditions. Numerical simulation results demonstrate cost improvement under two trajectory tracking weight settings. Experiments involving repeated contact between a robot wrist and a human hand demonstrate task-dependent adjustment of trajectory tracking accuracy and compliance.

**中文译文**

本文提出一种用于刚性机械臂的阻抗学习方法，通过重复交互调整轨迹跟踪精度与柔顺性之间的取舍。对于建模为参数未知的线性时变系统的环境，该方法结合梯度跟随与迭代改进，根据加权交互误差代价学习随时间变化的阻尼与刚度，无需精确辨识环境参数。自适应位置控制器通过跟踪目标阻抗生成的虚拟参考轨迹，实现所学阻抗。该控制器不需要机器人动力学的回归矩阵。对于所考虑的机械臂动力学，理论分析表明，当虚拟参考速度与加速度有界，且控制增益及自适应参数满足定理条件时，机械臂渐近跟踪虚拟参考轨迹，闭环信号保持有界。数值仿真结果表明，两种轨迹跟踪权重设置下的代价均得到改善。机器人腕部与人手重复接触的实验表明，轨迹跟踪精度与柔顺性能够按照任务要求进行调整。
