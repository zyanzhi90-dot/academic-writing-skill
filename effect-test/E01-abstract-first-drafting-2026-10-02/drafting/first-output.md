**English abstract**

Learning robot point-to-point motion from demonstrations requires reproducing the demonstrated motion while ensuring convergence to a target. Fitting demonstration data alone does not guarantee convergence from unfamiliar initial positions or after disturbances. Here, we propose a stable estimator of dynamical systems (SEDS) that learns a nonlinear autonomous dynamical system under stability constraints. A Gaussian mixture model represents the joint distribution of demonstrated positions and velocities, and Gaussian mixture regression yields a motion-generation function comprising state-dependent weighted combinations of local linear dynamical systems. SEDS optimizes the model parameters to fit the demonstrations while enforcing sufficient conditions for global asymptotic stability: all local systems share the target as an equilibrium, and the symmetric part of each local system matrix is negative definite. These conditions guarantee that the learned model converges asymptotically to the target from any initial state, with velocity tending to zero. Because motion generation depends on the current state rather than a prescribed time schedule, it can respond to spatial and temporal disturbances and continue towards the target. We evaluate the method in simulations of 20 human handwriting motions and in motion-reproduction and object-placement tasks using the iCub and Katana-T robot arms. The results demonstrate reproduction of the demonstrated position and velocity characteristics and generation of target-reaching motions from different initial positions. In the Katana-T experiments, moving the target during execution causes the generated trajectory to adjust towards the new target without relearning the model parameters.

**中文翻译**

从示范中学习机器人的点到点运动，需要在再现示范运动的同时确保运动收敛到目标。仅拟合示范数据并不能保证运动从示范以外的初始位置出发或受到扰动后仍能收敛。本文提出稳定动力系统估计器（SEDS），在稳定性约束下学习非线性自治动力系统。高斯混合模型描述示范中位置与速度的联合分布，高斯混合回归则得到由多个局部线性动力系统按状态相关权重组合而成的运动生成函数。SEDS优化模型参数以拟合示范，同时施加保证全局渐近稳定的充分条件：所有局部系统均以目标为共同平衡点，且各局部系统矩阵的对称部分均为负定。这些条件保证学习模型从任意初始状态渐近收敛到目标，速度也趋于零。由于运动生成依赖当前状态，而非预先安排的时间进度，因此能够响应空间和时间扰动，并继续朝目标运动。我们通过20种人类手写运动的仿真，以及使用iCub和Katana-T机械臂开展的运动再现与物体放置任务，对该方法进行评价。结果展示了对示范运动中位置和速度特点的再现，以及从不同初始位置生成到达目标的运动。在Katana-T实验中，执行期间移动目标后，生成的轨迹随之调整并朝新目标运动，无须重新学习模型参数。
