**English abstract**

This paper proposes an impedance learning method for rigid manipulators interacting with unknown, time-varying environments. The method adjusts desired damping and stiffness through repeated interactions according to a weighted cost of errors in position and the time integral of interaction force. Gradient following and iterative improvement are combined to update the impedance parameters without identifying an internal environment model. The learning is formulated for a linear time-varying mass–damper–spring environment. An adaptive position controller is designed to track the virtual reference trajectory generated by the desired impedance without requiring a regressor matrix for the robot dynamics. Theoretical analysis establishes asymptotic tracking of the virtual reference trajectory and bounded closed-loop signals under the stated dynamics, reference and gain conditions. Numerical simulation results demonstrate task-dependent impedance adaptation and reductions in the interaction error cost. Experiments with repeated wrist–hand contact further show that a higher trajectory-tracking weight increases stiffness and reduces position error, whereas a lower weight reduces stiffness and produces more compliant motion.

**中文翻译**

本文提出一种阻抗学习方法，用于刚性机械臂与未知、随时间变化的环境之间的交互。该方法通过重复交互调整期望阻尼和刚度，其依据是对位置误差和交互力时间积分误差进行加权的代价函数。梯度跟随与迭代改进相结合，在无需辨识环境内部模型的情况下更新阻抗参数。阻抗学习针对线性时变质量—阻尼—弹簧环境进行构建。自适应位置控制器用于跟踪由目标阻抗生成的虚拟参考轨迹，无需机器人动力学的回归矩阵。在所述动力学、参考轨迹及增益条件下，理论分析证明了实际位置对虚拟参考轨迹的渐近跟踪以及闭环信号的有界性。数值仿真结果展示了阻抗随任务要求的调整以及交互误差代价的降低。重复腕部—人手接触实验进一步表明，较高的轨迹跟踪权重使刚度增加、位置误差减小，而较低的权重使刚度降低、运动更加柔顺。
