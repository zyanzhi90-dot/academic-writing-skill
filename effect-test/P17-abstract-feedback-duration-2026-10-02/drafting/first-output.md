### Abstract

Learning a motion from human demonstrations does not by itself ensure that a robot can execute it accurately when its arm dynamics are uncertain. We present a skill-learning system that combines motion generation from repeated demonstrations with adaptive trajectory tracking. Gaussian mixture modeling and regression learn the nonlinear forcing term of a dynamic movement primitive from multiple demonstrations of the same task. The learned model generates reference trajectories whose shape is retained when the motion goal is changed. Changing the dynamic movement primitive’s time constant also adjusts motion duration while retaining the learned trajectory shape. A radial basis function neural-network controller tracks the resulting joint-space trajectories and compensates for uncertain arm dynamics. Under the stated model and controller conditions, the generated motion converges to its goal, while Lyapunov analysis establishes semiglobal uniform boundedness of the closed-loop signals. On a Baxter robot, motions learned from five pouring demonstrations were reproduced and adjusted to pour into another cup. In a drawing task, the robot drew curves smoother than five irregular demonstrations. In a separate circular-tracking test with a 0.94 kg load, neural-network learning reduced joint-position errors, which entered the range −0.04 to 0.04 rad. These results support learning and goal adjustment of the tested motions, together with tracking compensation under the tested load.

### 中文翻译

仅从人的动作示范中学到运动，并不能确保机器人在机械臂动力学存在不确定性时准确执行该运动。我们提出一种技能学习系统，将从多次示范中生成运动与自适应轨迹跟踪相结合。该系统利用高斯混合模型和高斯混合回归，从同一任务的多次示范中学习动态运动基元的非线性驱动项。学到的模型生成参考轨迹；改变运动目标后，轨迹仍保留原有的形状特征。改变动态运动基元的时间常数还可以调整运动持续时间，同时保留学到的轨迹形状特征。基于径向基函数神经网络的控制器跟踪由此得到的关节空间轨迹，并补偿机械臂动力学的不确定性。在所述模型和控制器条件下，生成的运动收敛至目标；李雅普诺夫分析则表明闭环信号半全局一致有界。在 Baxter 机器人上，系统再现了从五次倒水示范中学到的动作，并通过调整目标向另一只杯子倒水。在绘图任务中，机器人画出的曲线比五次不规则示范更平滑。在另一项携带 0.94 千克负载的圆形轨迹跟踪实验中，开启神经网络学习后，关节位置误差减小，并进入 −0.04 至 0.04 弧度的区间。这些结果支持在所测试运动中从示范学习并调整目标的能力，以及在所测试负载条件下的轨迹跟踪补偿效果。
