**English abstract**

Learning point-to-point robot motions from demonstrations requires reproducing the demonstrated motion while ensuring convergence to a target. Data fitting alone does not guarantee convergence from new initial states or after perturbations. We propose the Stable Estimator of Dynamical Systems (SEDS) to learn nonlinear autonomous motion models under stability constraints. A Gaussian mixture model represents the joint distribution of states and their rates of change, and Gaussian mixture regression combines local linear dynamical systems using state-dependent weights. SEDS optimizes the model parameters to fit the demonstrations while imposing a common target equilibrium and negative-definite symmetric parts of the local system matrices. These sufficient conditions guarantee global asymptotic stability of the learned model at the target, with the rate of change tending to zero. The model generates motion from the current state rather than a prescribed time schedule, allowing it to respond to spatial and temporal perturbations during execution. We evaluate the method on 20 handwriting motions in simulation and on the iCub and Katana-T robot arms. The robot experiments demonstrate reproduction of position and velocity characteristics and motion generation toward the target from different initial positions. In the Katana-T experiments, the generated trajectories adjust to a target moved during execution.

**中文翻译**

从示范中学习机器人的点到点运动，需要在再现示范运动特点的同时确保运动收敛到目标。仅拟合数据并不能保证模型从新的初始状态出发或受到扰动后仍收敛到目标。我们提出稳定动力系统估计器（SEDS），在稳定性约束下学习非线性自治运动模型。高斯混合模型描述状态及其变化率的联合分布，高斯混合回归则利用随状态变化的权重组合多个局部线性动力系统。SEDS 优化模型参数以拟合示范，同时约束各局部系统具有共同的目标平衡点，且其系统矩阵的对称部分为负定。这些充分条件保证学习得到的模型在目标处全局渐近稳定，状态变化率也趋于零。模型根据当前状态生成运动，而不依赖预先安排的时间进度，因此能够在执行过程中响应空间和时间扰动。我们通过20种手写运动的模拟以及 iCub 和 Katana-T 机械臂实验评价该方法。机器人实验展示了对示范运动的位置和速度特点的再现，以及从不同初始位置生成朝向目标的运动。在 Katana-T 实验中，目标在执行期间移动后，生成的轨迹相应调整。
